## About jiachao-supply-chain

**jiachao-supply-chain** develops software for POD (print-on-demand) commerce, with a focus on pattern assets, custom artwork workflows and multi-site operations.

Our projects span asset libraries, artwork requests, order collaboration and AI-assisted image processing. We also explore how asset editing and design tools can work together.

## Project areas

| Area | What we work on |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Asset libraries & sites** | Organizing, finding and presenting pattern assets, with multi-site applications for browsing, purchasing and downloading them. |
| **Artwork operations** | Business and collaboration tools for artwork requests, orders, reference images, attachments and delivery materials. |
| **AI image tools** | Developing and integrating image editing, pattern extraction, prompt generation and image search capabilities. |
| **POD creative workspace** | Exploring asset organization, lightweight editing and collaboration with design software. This work remains at the prototype-validation stage. |

## How the projects fit together

Asset libraries and business tools organize assets, requests and orders, while sites provide the content and user-facing entry points. AI tools focus on image processing; the creative workspace explores asset editing and design collaboration.

> Starting with patterns, we develop tools around assets, creative work and business workflows.
